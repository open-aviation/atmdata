This dataset was created for the EUROCONTROL Performance Review Commission
Data Challenge 2024 on aircraft take-off weight estimation.

It combines flight information, ADS-B trajectory data from OpenSky, and actual
take-off weight data from airlines. The dataset contains 527,162 flights flown
throughout Europe in 2022, representing 6.1% of all flights in EUROCONTROL
airspace.
