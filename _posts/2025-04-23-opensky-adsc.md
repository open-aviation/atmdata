ADS-C data collected by the OpenSky Network since 7 July 2023.

The dataset accompanies the paper "A First Look at Exploiting the Automatic
Dependent Surveillance-Contract Protocol for Open Aviation Research" and
includes decoded ADS-C data together with a file structure description.
