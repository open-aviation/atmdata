Dataset for the EUROCONTROL Performance Review Commission Data Challenge 2025
on aircraft fuel burn estimation.

The challenge is to predict aircraft fuel consumption during flight intervals
using ADS-B trajectory data, ACARS fuel telemetry, and flight metadata. The
dataset contains around 15,000 flights from April to October 2025, covering 27
aircraft types across global routes.
